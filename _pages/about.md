I’m Shreeram Bhattarai, and I’m someone who’s absolutely fascinated by science—especially the mind-blowing world of quantum computation. I’ve always been a curious soul, the kind of person who’s constantly wondering about the big questions: What’s out there? How does it all work? That curiosity has led me down some pretty exciting paths, and right now, I’m pouring most of my time and energy into exploring quantum computation.
<hr>
For me, it’s not just a passing interest—it’s something I’m determined to dig into and understand on a deeper level. Every day feels like a new chance to learn something amazing about it. I’ve been soaking up knowledge wherever I can find it: watching YouTube lectures (some of those professors explain things in ways that just click!), flipping through books that break down the complex stuff, and getting hands-on by learning quantum programming with the Qiskit framework. It’s challenging, sure, but that’s part of what makes it so fun—I’m piecing it together bit by bit, and I love seeing how it all connects.
<hr>
I’m really excited to share this journey with anyone who’s curious like me. Whether you’re into quantum tech or just love exploring new ideas, I’d love to chat about it—or maybe even learn something new from you!
