I've been in this world for a while now, and for most of that time, I was a student who never asked "why." I learned whatever was put in front of me without questioning the purpose. Why are we learning this specific topic? Why does this concept exist?

I studied plenty of mathematical formulas and equations. I knew how to use them and where they fit into a test, but I never truly understood why they were there or how they came into existence. To be honest, those equations didn't really mean anything to me; they were just a part of my studies that I accepted as they were.

Once I finished university and started working, that mindset actually served me well. In the industry, often all that matters is knowing what tools are available and how to use them to get a result. Nothing more.

But after a few years of working, I started to feel like I was living on the surface. Everything felt shallow. That's when I started to dive deeper. I began looking into complex topics—specifically quantum mechanics—and slowly, the "why" behind the theories and equations started to click.

For the first time, things are actually making sense.

My curiosity is pulling me deeper, and I've realized I want to understand how it all works at the core. I know this takes time and a level of dedication that doesn't always fit into this "fast-paced" world. Our schools and universities are designed to help us keep up and survive, regardless of whether we truly understand the material or not.

I'm changing my direction now. I'm choosing to go deeper, even if it takes longer. For the first time, I'm not just following a script—I'm enjoying the process of discovery. Let's see how far I can go.
