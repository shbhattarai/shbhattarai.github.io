It's been a while , I am contiously searching something. I am always looking other than what I have right now or whatever I am doing. I don't know why but I am not satisfied whatever I am doing now. It's not that I don't like it but I am looking something which can fullfill my thirst to know out there. What is life? Who we are ? what we are doing here? What I am doing and why I am doing? What am I searching for? like these there are so many things going on my mind. So I am creating this site to explore myself. 
<hr>

I am kind of a person who wants to know almost everything, I want to know mathematics, physics, computation, neuroscience, chemistry , biology, spirtuality and so on. I am not sure if my life length could be enough to do so.
